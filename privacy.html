Privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

What Habit Grid is

Habit Grid is a personal habit, mood and motivation tracker at habit-gri.netlify.app.

Information we collect

  • Your tracker data: habit names and emoji, daily ticks, and mood and motivation scores you enter.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture, which Google shares so the app can show which account you are using.

We do not collect your Google contacts, files, calendar or any other Google data.

Where it is stored

Without an account, everything stays in your browser on your device. When you sign in, a copy is stored with Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, Germany) so it can sync across your devices. Only your signed-in account can read or change your data.

How it is used

Your data is used only to run the tracker and sync it between your devices. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking.

Deleting your data

Sign out and clear the site's data in your browser to remove it from a device. To delete your cloud copy and account, email adenitantun@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days.
